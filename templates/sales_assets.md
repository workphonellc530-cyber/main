# Sales Assets (Copy-and-Use)

## 1) Cold Email Template

**Subject options**
- Found hidden booked-revenue in your pipeline
- Quick idea to recover lost appointments this month
- 14-day revenue recovery system for {{company}}

**Email**

Hi {{first_name}},

I looked at {{company}} and noticed a common pattern we fix for {{niche}} teams:
valuable leads arrive, but speed-to-lead lag and no-shows quietly drain revenue.

We deploy a 14-day AI system that:
1) responds to inbound leads immediately,
2) confirms/reminds appointments automatically, and
3) reactivates missed and dormant leads.

If it helps, I can share a quick estimate of what this might recover each month for {{company}}.

Open to a short call this week?

{{your_name}}

---

## 2) Discovery Call Script (15 Minutes)

1. "Walk me through how new leads currently get handled in the first 10 minutes."
2. "What percentage of booked appointments no-show or reschedule?"
3. "How many dormant leads are untouched in your CRM?"
4. "If we recovered even 15-25% of this leakage, what would that mean monthly?"
5. "Would you like the 14-day rollout and pricing now?"

---

## 3) Close Script

"Based on your numbers, the recoverable revenue is roughly {{estimated_recovery}}/month.
Our setup is {{setup_fee}} with {{upfront_ratio}} collected at signing so we can begin deployment immediately.

If we start this week, you should see measurable lift in appointments within days.
Would you like me to send the agreement and payment link now so we secure your onboarding slot?"

---

## 4) Proposal Structure (One Page)

1. Current leakage snapshot
2. 14-day implementation milestones
3. KPI targets (response speed, show rate, booked appointments)
4. Pricing and payment terms
5. Start date + onboarding checklist

---

## 5) Referral Ask Script

"I have two onboarding slots left this month for businesses that want to recover missed appointment revenue quickly.
Do you know 1-2 owners/operators who should see this?"

---

## 6) Follow-Up Sequence

- Day 0: Initial email + LinkedIn touch
- Day 2: ROI angle follow-up
- Day 4: Case-based proof or analog
- Day 7: Capacity-close message
- Day 10: Final follow-up with specific start date
