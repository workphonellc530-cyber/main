# 14-Day Revenue Sprint Blueprint

## Objective

Generate at least **$200,000 profit** in 14 days by selling a high-ticket AI deployment offer with upfront cash collection.

## Offer: "Revenue Recovery AI Sprint"

### Promise
Recover missed revenue in 14 days using:
1. Instant speed-to-lead automation
2. AI appointment confirmation and no-show reduction
3. Missed-call text back + reactivation engine

### Commercial Terms (Default)
- Setup: **$25,000**
- Upfront on signature: **80% ($20,000)**
- Monthly retainer: **$3,000**
- Capacity cap: 12 onboarding slots this month

## Why This Offer Sells Fast

- Pain is immediate and quantifiable (lost appointments and lead leakage).
- ROI can be projected from existing lead data on the first call.
- Delivery risk is low because implementation can start with existing channels (SMS/email/calls).

## Core Math

To clear $200,000 profit with default costs:

- Fixed costs for sprint: $16,500
- Cash per signed deal: $20,000
- Required deals: 11

If 2 deals come from warm referrals, outbound must close 9 deals.

## Daily KPI Cadence

- Outreach/day: 143
- Replies/day: 12
- Meetings/day: 4
- Shows/day: 3
- Closes/day: 1

These are minimums. Execution should target 20-30% above minimum.

## 14-Day Timeline

### Days 1-2: Infrastructure + First Outreach
- Finalize niche and territory list.
- Upload target accounts into outreach queue.
- Launch first outreach wave and referral asks.

### Days 3-5: Discovery + First Closes
- Run discovery calls daily.
- Deliver same-day ROI map and proposal.
- Close first clients and collect deposits.

### Days 6-8: Pipeline Expansion
- Run second and third outbound waves.
- Publish limited-capacity urgency.
- Add partner channel (agency, consultant, media buyer referrals).

### Days 9-11: Close Sprint
- Focus on proposal follow-up and objection handling.
- Use CFO-friendly ROI summaries.
- Bundle annual terms or additional automations for larger upfront payments.

### Days 12-14: Final Recovery and Closeout
- Re-engage stalled opportunities with deadline-based offers.
- Conduct executive follow-up calls.
- Finalize signatures and collect remaining deposits.

## Sales Call Structure (15-25 min)

1. Current revenue leak diagnosis
2. Dollarized opportunity estimate
3. 14-day implementation map
4. Risk reversal and milestone commitments
5. Signature + deposit request

## Objection Handling Framework

- "Need to think": "What KPI would make this a clear yes right now?"
- "Too expensive": show no-show + speed-to-lead loss against monthly recoverable value.
- "Need partner approval": offer executive summary with strict 48-hour onboarding window.

## Risk Management

- Track conversion rates daily and compare to model.
- If reply rate drops, rotate messaging and improve niche targeting.
- If close rate drops, tighten qualification criteria and sharpen ROI proof.

## Non-Negotiables

- Same-day proposal delivery for every qualified discovery call.
- Daily follow-up on every open proposal.
- Deposit collection link sent live on close call.
