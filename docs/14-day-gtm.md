## 14-day GTM sprint (aiming for $200k — not guaranteed)

This is a **high-intensity sales + delivery sprint** designed to make a $200k outcome *plausible* via **setup fees** (cash now), not by hoping for SaaS MRR in two weeks.

### The core strategy

- **Sell high-ticket setups** for one niche where response time directly impacts revenue.
- Use this app as the **delivery vehicle** (playbooks + audit trail + billing).
- Deliver fast, get proof, then upsell to retainers/referrals.

### Offer (copy/paste)

**Name**: Revenue Response System  
**Promise**: Reduce lead-response time to <5 minutes and increase booked calls / reduce support load using an AI playbook console + audit trail.  
**Delivery**: 7 days.  
**Price** (pick one):

- **$20,000 setup** (goal: 10 closes)
- **$10,000 setup** (goal: 20 closes)
- **$7,500 setup** + **$2,500/mo** retainer (goal: 8–12 closes)

**Scope (what they get in 7 days)**:

- Deployed console (this app) under your branding
- 3 tailored playbooks
  - Lead qualification
  - Support autoresponder
  - Proposal writer
- “Run history” proof dashboard for weekly optimization

**What’s explicitly out-of-scope** (so you can ship fast):

- Full inbox automation, CRM writebacks, and multi-agent toolchains (Phase 2 upsell)

### Pick a niche (choose ONE)

Pick a niche where *minutes* matter and inbound volume exists:

- Agencies doing paid ads / web design / SEO (inbound leads leak)
- Home services (plumbing/roofing/HVAC) (missed calls = lost jobs)
- Clinics (dental/medspa) (no-shows + reschedules)
- Shopify brands (support load + delivery issues)
- B2B SaaS with demo requests (lead routing + qualification)

### Daily checklist

#### Day 1
- Define your niche + ICP + one-line promise
- Create a lead list (100–300 targets)
- Set pricing + “7-day deliverables” as a 1-page doc

#### Day 2
- Customize the demo:
  - Update landing copy to the niche
  - Prepare 3 example inputs that match their real messages
- Build a 5-minute Loom demo flow:
  - inbound message → playbook output → run history → billing

#### Day 3–4
- Outbound volume + follow-ups
  - Goal: **100–200 messages/day**
  - Channel mix: cold email + LinkedIn + warm intros
- Book 10–20 diagnostics

#### Day 5–7
- Run diagnostics + close pilots
  - Sell “limited slots” for next 7 days
  - Collect setup fee upfront
- Start delivery immediately for first closes

#### Day 8–10
- Deliver setups in batches
- Ask for permission to screenshot run history as proof
- Publish 1 case study post (anonymous if needed)

#### Day 11–14
- Upsell retainers + Phase 2 integrations
- Harvest referrals (“2 people like you”)

### Cold email template (short + high conversion)

Subject: `{{first}} — quick question about your lead response`

Body:
> Hey {{first}} — quick question.  
> If I could help you respond + qualify inbound leads in <5 minutes (without adding headcount) and give you an audit trail to prove ROI, would you want to see a 5‑minute demo?  
> If yes: what’s your weekly lead volume and current time-to-first-response?

Follow-up (48h):
> Worth a quick look? If not you, who owns lead response at {{company}}?

### Diagnostic call structure (15 minutes)

- **Minute 0–3**: quantify current state
  - lead volume/week
  - time-to-first-response
  - close rate / booked-call rate
- **Minute 3–8**: show the demo for their exact message type
- **Minute 8–12**: pricing + 7-day deliverables
- **Minute 12–15**: close
  - “If we start Monday, you’ll have it live by next Monday. Want me to send the invoice and kickoff checklist?”

### Risk reversal that doesn’t kill you

- “If we don’t ship the console + 3 tailored playbooks in 7 days, you don’t pay the final 30%.”

### Compliance / ethics (important)

- Don’t spam. Follow CAN-SPAM/GDPR and platform ToS.
- Don’t claim guaranteed results; sell a **process + deliverables**.
